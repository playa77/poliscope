checks canonical account, claimant, holder, scope and dated-time identities before
calling actual DOM-05 care. It accepts the complete economy candidate only when
population and work resolve, and compares the second pure DOM-02 evaluation with
the actual care report's evaluation. Both start from the original book; neither
spends the other's staged output. No competing allocator or quantity formula is
introduced. The complete request/world/units/runtime identity binds opaque tokens;
the consumer recomputes and checks the producer token. Prior hosts receive new
declarations, source and fixture identities only, without callbacks or reviews.

The synthetic literal fixture represents 10 canonical people in each of two
arrangements and 10 in their union. One 12 h budget supplies care 3 h (including
1 h already posted), training 3 h and employment 6 h, leaving 2 h unmet employment.

entitlements. Care denial preserves its already used hour and unused reserved
hour; neither unmet duties nor membership overlap makes new time available.
