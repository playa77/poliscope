## Scope and acceptance

A single explicit scoped decision gates selected economic claims. Governance
administration and independent first-stage work spend the exact same initial
finite book as the subsequent economic allocation. Both stages name every claim;
future economic claims are explicitly closed in administration, and earlier
claims are explicitly closed in the later stage. Identical holders, dates, claims,
postings, reservations and subtotals prevent independent duplicated budgets.
Governance uses actual shared policy resolution and ballots. Its fully approved
outcome is a prerequisite only: it cannot override unknown/denied eligibility,
create rights, currency, goods or time, change the host's numerical terms or apply
a policy. Every gated claim must reference the proposal's affected canonical
account. All this remains a private counterfactual preview, never policy adoption.

Unknown authority/ballots/administration withhold the final candidate. Rejection,
no quorum and insufficient administration block gated claims, retain their
causes and preserve independent spent work. Earlier completed claims and prior
postings survive once; holds remain effective. Host chooses phase precedence
explicitly; queue order never chooses an authority. Separate budget, physical
stock and entitlement evidence stays visible.
