## Frozen fixture and independent arithmetic

`tests/engine/headless_fixture.py` assembles a synthetic world directly from
production contracts and the published mechanism declaration fixture. It imports
no existing implementation-test builders. Two settlements expose overlapping
views of the same canonical resource.
Allocation uses one east-scope stock and one dated labor account; both competing
mechanism instances bind that canonical accounting scope. Cross-scope delivery
mechanisms remain domain work; the west view does not create another stock.
Four overlapping access views refer to the same canonical water resource; two institutions have explicit jurisdiction. The authored
priority/access rule is test content, never a default civic arrangement.

Before execution, the expected ledger is fixed independently:

- Stock: 100 opening + 10 finite imports − 10 initial loss = 100 available.
- B holds 40 of that stock. A requests 80 but can receive only 60; B receives 40.
  Unmet demand is 20 and 40 respectively; neither claim receives its request.
- Each consumed kg uses 0.1 labor hours in `day:0`: 12 − 6 − 4 = 2 hours left.
  No accrual or period conversion is inferred.
- Yield is 0.7 product + 0.2 residual + 0.1 process loss per actual kg:
  70 product + 20 residual + 10 process loss = 100 consumed stock.
  Closing stock and outstanding reservation are both zero.
- Access denied to A leaves B's 80 fulfilled, stock 20, labor 4, product 56,
  residual 16 and process loss 8. B's reservation is consumed once.
- Canonical union across east/watershed/region is 100 kg, never 300 kg.
- A labor budget of 5 permits only 50 kg of total consumption. The outstanding
  reservation must continue to constrain spendable stock independently of labor.

Quantum is 0.01, maximum 1000. Precision violations must be rejected before
publication. Unsupported qualitative choices carry no invented numeric result.
Optional goals change observation/report identity, never physical evaluation.
All expected ledger literals precede measurement; no production accounting helper
is used as the oracle. Alternative inputs and immutable accepted trace closures
must survive undo, new futures, cache eviction and model-free reopening.
