claim. Full commitment to every named prerequisite is required. Slots remain
disjoint from the chain's material, time, energy, land, throughput and rights.
No response-slot/kg conversion, free recovery or unlimited external aid is inferred.
The host explicitly chooses dependencies, scope mapping and queue precedence.
Capacity readiness cannot grant access or remove an independent unknown.

candidate. Known denied/delayed authority, withheld disclosure, declined
participation, ineligibility, missing access, offline capacity or unavailable aid
block mapped claims and retain distinct evidence. Prior commitments and holds
survive once without proving current permissions. Independent work and committed
inputs survive later blocked gates. Reject incomplete/duplicate/foreign mappings,
account collisions, mismatched dates, malformed canonical references, forged DTOs,
stale request/world context and foreign runtime identities. Common conformance
