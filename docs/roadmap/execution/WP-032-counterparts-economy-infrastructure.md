## Implementation and separate author inspection

The pair calls the actual DOM-08 facility evaluator, requiring canonical account,
claimant, period and local scope references. Facility and economy account/claim IDs
are disjoint; every economy claim explicitly names its demands or independence.
Original economy terms are validated before staging, even when reach is unknown.
Full named reach preserves original terms; known shortfall or denial restricts
eligibility. Unknown eligibility and terms remain unknown even behind a known
block. Facility commitments stay visible, including partial commitments and prior
use. No slot-to-goods/time conversion, synchronized delivery or refund is inferred.
Economy always starts from its original book; pure preflight outputs are discarded.

The versioned literal synthetic fixture uses one canonical cohort's 12 h: care 3
(including 1 prior), training 3, employment 6 (2 unmet). Five existing entitlements
permit 5 kg goods from 20 kg (3 unmet), leaving 15 kg. Four one-slot demands share
facility, bridge and road accounts of 5 slots each, leaving 1 each. Care explicitly
declares independence. Independent finite feasible-set enumeration and Fraction
balance constraints cover slot scarcity, queue orders, both procedures, stock and
entitlement shortages. Retained care and slot holds, both-book replay and known
denial preserve postings once. Two local scopes and a duplicate same-cohort budget
case check that geography cannot create time.
