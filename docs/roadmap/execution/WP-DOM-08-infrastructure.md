## Scope and acceptance

Complete a bounded host-only static facility/network capacity interface. Canonical
assets occupy canonical locations; directed links bind canonical connectivity
relationships. Every asset has one dated facility-slot capacity account; facility
and link roles cannot duplicate an asset or account. All mapped accounts share
one scope/period and unit. Explicit simple paths consume one unit on each link
and at the facility through the existing allocator. No route optimization or
implicit precedence. A graph-disconnected beneficiary cannot consume a surplus
facility. Known planned/offline/retired assets supply no additional capacity;
unknown availability withholds evaluation. Access denial remains distinct from
physical disconnection. Retain original claims, postings, reservations, topology,
prior/additional/unmet values, scope, units and period in detached evidence.

Validate independent finite integer feasibility, shared links/facilities across
school and clinic claims, local/remote/disconnected paths, lifecycle/access and
unknown inputs, exact quantum handling, prior holds/replay, malformed canonical
links and DTOs, and actual education/health consumer evidence. Run full Python,
contracts/content, desktop unit/roadmap/typecheck and Linux package/smoke where
available. Separate author inspection precedes commit/push/regular PR delivery.
