## Implementation and separate author inspection

Merged prerequisite witnesses: DOM-05 care arrangements PR #57 at
`6539b98cdb4f25ccabd5bfc61f0f7f9c36d432a7`, DOM-01/02 real counterpart PR #74
at the base above, and the earlier shared allocator/membership/conformance services
retained by those merges. This allocation does not accept their DOM/WP parents.

The pair reparses nested requests, requires identical care/provisioning work
inputs, validates the existing economy chain context, and invokes actual care
membership/obligation evaluation. Only resolved population and care work allow
the real DOM-01/02 chain. The pure allocator recomputes the same initial book;
its output must exactly equal the care work evaluation. It never allocates from
an already-consumed care book a second time. Final evidence preserves all phase
postings, reservations, claims, beneficiaries and original source references.
Denied care leaves its prior actual hour and two unmet hours explicit; denied
goods leaves available stock. Neither denial is treated as unknown or shortage.
Care results establish finite work only, not goods delivered to a care service,
health improvement, demographic prediction or an arrangement-quality score.

The independently authored fixture extends the retained finite chain with two
canonical arrangements sharing one ten-person cohort. Each arrangement counts ten;
the union counts ten. One time account supplies 12 h: care 3 (including a prior
1 h posting), training 3, production 5 and freight 1. Processing 10 kg yields
7 kg food, 2 kg residual and 1 kg loss. Freight/storage move 2 kg; entitlement
permits 1 kg use. Opening mass 20 equals retained 18 plus loss 1 plus use 1.
A separate small-integer oracle varies care/training requests; population variants
prove counts do not multiply either people or time. These are synthetic capacity
inputs, not population-derived care requirements or empirical coefficients.

The two trusted callbacks run actual care and actual provisioning. Matching label
ports contain opaque context-bound evidence digests; no book is deserialized,
no mutable staging cache exists, and no numerical account is publicly published.
Unknown care/population prevents a producer book; unknown chain inputs prevent
a final candidate. The shared evaluator represents these as `missing_input`
observations, retaining `incomplete` outcome. Resolved partial declarations retain
`partial` observations and `incomplete` outcome. Direct reports separately retain
actual/unmet work, population and phase/account evidence.

ready and 42 pairs stay pending. Registries carry both pairs' declarations/source
