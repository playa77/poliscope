## Implementation and separate author inspection

The actual health evaluator consumes beneficiary care, clinical staff work and
supply claims through the existing shared service-capacity/economy interfaces.
The local health work request must equal the exact local account/holder
projection of the pair's initial work book, including all claims, holds, prior
postings, activity terms, dated periods and subtotal views. Remote stock and
entitlement accounts stay in the complete book. Only the newly staged local
postings continue into the existing provisioning evaluator. Preexisting remote
work claims/postings are outside this bounded projection contract. No numerical
rules or resource stores are copied. Facility account/claim identities cannot
collide with any production/freight/storage/use pool; its local posting namespace
is never merged.

Explicit health policy and port reviews remain separate host inputs. Policy
resolution invokes the real WP-013 resolver, without policy callbacks. Context
binds request, world/membership, units/runtime, model/catalog and complete policy
registry/review evidence. Opaque label ports contain digests, not executable or
serialized resource books. Unknown policy, eligibility, qualification, time,
supply or facility capacity withholds a final candidate. Independent commitments
remain visible when another prerequisite blocks readiness. Need, exposure and
selected health-state references remain bound descriptive inputs; changing them
cannot manufacture numerical health effects.

The synthetic fixture starts with 14 hours and 20 kg. Ordinary care consumes 3 h
(including one prior posting), beneficiary care 3 h and clinical work 2 h in the
same canonical cohort/time account. Supplies consume 2 kg. Production consumes
5 h and 10 kg, yielding 7 kg food, 2 residual and 1 loss; freight consumes the
remaining hour to move 2 kg. Storage preserves those goods, and explicit remote
entitlement permits final use 1 kg. Independent integer/Fraction oracles vary
beneficiary/staff/supply demand and reconcile time and physical mass. A separate
3-slot clinic pool allocates two beneficiary slots (one prior) plus one other
slot, leaving one other claim unmet. Capacity variants 2–5 check that pool without
changing physical goods. These are input commitments, not delivered treatment.
