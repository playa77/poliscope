## Implementation and separate author inspection

The pair invokes actual scoped governance, stages its complete account batch,
then invokes actual education prerequisites. Original requests share the exact
work book, holders, period and scope. Decision claims and all service student,
staff and supply claims are disjoint and explicitly closed in the other phase.
All services supply one approval-dependence declaration. Required services name
the decision target and proposal-affected work and facility accounts. Detached
original-term education preflight validates all service and facility inputs even
when an unknown decision prevents staging; its allocations are discarded.

Known nonapproval denies new dependent work, slots and service eligibility while
preserving original unknowns. Explicit independent services can still proceed.
Approval preserves original access, eligibility, qualification, policy resolution
and obligations. There is no learning prediction, attendance, qualification grant
or automatic policy adoption. Facility slots remain a disjoint finite budget.
Unknowns retain phase evidence and withhold the complete candidate. Prior fulfilled
work and seats survive replay and later rejection without refunds or double use.

student 3, staff 2 and employment 1. One prior decision hour and one prior care hour
are retained once. Stock 20 kg leaves 16 after 2 supplies and 2 goods; four rights
units and three facility slots remain finite. Independent integer feasible sets
and Fraction balances cover budgets 4–20 under priority and all-or-nothing rules.
Multiple services compete for the same book/slots; explicit independence survives
nonapproval. Tests exercise two local scopes, region and overlapping authority
with explicit selection, plus duplicate same-cohort dated time-pool rejection.
