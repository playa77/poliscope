## Implementation and separate author inspection

The pair calls actual DOM-03 governance, stages its complete account batch, then
calls actual DOM-04 adjudication. Both original requests must share the same
batch, holders and period. Decision work and case work are disjoint; future work
is explicitly closed in the first phase and decision work is closed in the second.
Every case supplies one approval-dependence declaration. Required cases must name
the decision target and proposal-affected work accounts. Each evaluator retains
its own scoped authority resolution, jurisdiction and selected binding. All original
terms undergo detached preflight; these temporary allocations are discarded.

Approval only preserves legal work access. Known nonapproval denies new mapped
work without replacing unknown access or terms. Explicit independent cases can
still proceed. Case eligibility, disposition, rationale and obligations remain host
inputs; neither vote nor work allocation invents legal merits. Completed prior
case work survives later rejection, without refunds or duplicate postings.
Unresolved decision or legal prerequisites retain phase evidence and withhold a
complete candidate. There is no policy adoption or enforcement.
