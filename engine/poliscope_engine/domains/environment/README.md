# Finite ecological prerequisites v1

`evaluate_environment` stages private commitments through the shared allocator
and canonical `AccountBook`. It owns no world state and performs no IO. Host
inputs map each resource/role once to an extraction stock or dated sink allowance.
Direct resource/scope references retain local and overlapping views. Weights do
not grant capacity or multiply stock. All competing scopes share one account.

Access, physical estimates and selected limits are separate inputs. Unknown
physical boundaries/access withhold all new commitments. Explicit denied access
retains unmet claims. Finite-boundary shortfalls and access ceilings have separate
findings. A selected limit diagnoses gross outward postings in the retained book,
including unclaimed prior debits; imports do not cancel those debits. Reservations
affect availability, not completed debit totals. Unknown/unset limits are distinct
and do not change allocations. Incomplete candidates label retained-only totals.

Extraction and sink claims are independent prerequisite commitments. Sink
allowances do not demonstrate physical absorption, disposal or health effects.
No regeneration, pollution concentration, exposure, land-use change, common drought
draw, transition, delivery into provisioning, counterpart acceptance or source
activation is inferred. The host supplies units, estimates, explicit precedence
and the complete canonical book. This adapter cannot discover omitted competitors
or validate separate calls against original openings as one integrated result.

The authored card records the hypothetical procedure and independent oracle.
`exploration.interview` offers explicit retain/reverse queue alternatives with no
selected default. `preview` binds the complete request, world graph, unit registry
and implementation identities, changes only claim order and returns detached
private evidence. It never applies or publishes the candidate book.

`coverage.validate_coverage` reconciles every DOM-10 branch/leaf and pinned
source/system choice against the immutable inventory. Partial coverage is finite
prerequisite accounting only; remaining DOM-10 parent requirements stay open.
