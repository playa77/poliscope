# Scoped equal-member decision preview

`evaluate_governance(request, graph=..., mechanisms=..., units=...)` accepts a
complete trusted host snapshot. It validates direct jurisdiction and the exact
proposal/target through the production WP-013 policy resolver. Overlapping
bindings retain named findings; file or identifier order never chooses authority.
An explicit select rule must select the proposal being considered. Composition
and bounds remain policy services, outside this ballot procedure.

The host declares canonical eligible cohort/institution IDs, one equal vote per
member, quorum, and strict majority or unanimity. Duplicate/ineligible ballots
are rejected. Abstentions count toward quorum and the approval denominator;
missing members do not. Ties reject, missing quorum has a separate outcome, and
unknown ballots/rules/inputs give a null outcome. These assumptions are authored
hypothetical procedures, not empirical legitimacy claims or population weighting.
The host must validate disjoint representation, delegate mandates and eligibility;
registry existence alone does not establish those institutional facts.

Named administrative claims must be positive participation work in the selected
scope with registered population-cohort claimants. Their actual time is allocated
by DOM-02's existing holder/period/dimension checks and shared finite allocator.
Care, training and other activities compete in the declared full claim order.
Every required administrative claim must be fully fulfilled before a decision
can complete; a partial hour allocation does not produce a fractional decision.
Earlier fulfillment remains inspectable and is not deducted again.

Results retain the detached full request, policy findings/rules/provenance and
complete economy account/claim/posting evidence. Allocation batches represent
private demand previews, including for rejected or capacity-blocked proposals.
No result is a revision candidate or authorization to adopt a policy, spend time,
publish or execute institutional action. There is no store, clock, callback,
renderer/IPC, default civic answer, global legitimacy score or transition here.

[Model card](decision-procedure-v1.json) records the bounded authored procedure.
[Decision package record](../../../../docs/roadmap/execution/WP-DOM-03-decision-procedure.md)
retains the original procedure acceptance.

## Approval exploration and source coverage

`exploration.interview(request, graph=..., mechanisms=..., units=..., scope_id=...)`
returns authored strict-majority and unanimity Definitions without a selected
option. `preview(..., edit=GovernanceEdit(...))` changes only the approval rule.
The digest binds the complete request, world and jurisdiction, mechanism/model
identities and reviews, unit registries and semantic runtime versions. Stale
inputs, foreign options/scopes and local edits over multi-scope competing work
are rejected. Canonical representation, eligibility, quorum, ballots, mandates
and work precedence remain explicit host prerequisites. Neither option is a
population-weighted vote or a model of consensus-building.

`report(...)` retains the detached full GovernanceEvaluation and the existing
DOM-02 report, exposing requested/prior/incremental/unmet work, holder, cohort,
scope, dated period, unit and posting links. Repeated shared time availability is
context, not additive capacity. Governance and administration statuses remain
separate: an unknown ballot or authority conflict withholds the decision outcome
while known administrative demand can still be inspected. Unknown administrative
inputs yield null incremental measurements and no complete allocation batch.
All demand batches are private previews even after an approved decision.

[Research inventory](../../../../content/governance/coverage-v1.json) reconciles
all three branches, nine leaves and 25 historical source/system decisions against
the immutable source map. Five catalog rows have partial hypothetical support;
all decisions remain unreviewed provenance. Unsupported representation,
deliberation, expertise, oversight, disclosure and corruption effects stay gaps.
No source policy, production content, revision, database or renderer is activated.
[Exploration package record](../../../../docs/roadmap/execution/WP-DOM-03-options-coverage.md)
retains validation and limitations. Other actual counterpart fixtures and all
parent/static, transition, Windows and scientific/cumulative gates remain open.

The economy-owned [DOM-02/03 counterpart](../economy/governance.py) uses actual
governance outcomes as explicit access prerequisites. Administration and subsequent
economic work share one dated finite account book. Rejection, missing quorum and
capacity shortage remain distinguishable; conflicting or unknown decisions
withhold a complete candidate. Approval remains a counterfactual prerequisite,
never policy adoption or permission to override existing access restrictions.
