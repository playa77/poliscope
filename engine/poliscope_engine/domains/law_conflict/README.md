# Scoped legal case and capacity preview

`evaluate_adjudication(request, graph=..., mechanisms=..., units=...)` accepts a
complete trusted host snapshot and returns detached evidence only. Each case
retains its claimant cohort, right reference, responsible institution, explicit
eligibility, incompatible-right references, proposed disposition/rationale and
proposed obligations. Referenced rights and duties are host assertions, not
validated law. The procedure evaluates whether the declared ruling can complete;
it does not derive legal merits or grant/revoke a right.

Direct jurisdiction and the exact case binding/target use shared world and
WP-013 authority resolution, also used by DOM-03. Conflicting authority remains
an institutional finding until an explicit resolution selects that binding.
Composed/bounded policy values cannot silently authorize a different ruling.
Unknown eligibility, unresolved incompatible rights, missing disposition/rationale
or unknown policy withhold the outcome. Other cases retain independent findings.

Each case names unique, positive participation claims of canonical worker
cohorts in its scope. DOM-02 validates dated time budgets, holder identity,
units, requirements and the complete explicit claim order. Required work must
be fully fulfilled; partial work cannot produce fractional adjudication. One work
claim cannot complete two cases. Care, governance administration, training and
other work compete in the same batch. The host supplies distinct work IDs for
distinct tasks and canonical disjoint cohorts. A legal claimant is not necessarily
the adjudicating worker. Case list/identifier order cannot change work precedence.

Known eligible cases with insufficient work return `capacity_blocked`; the right,
unmet work and proposed obligation remain visible. `ineligible` identifies a
host-declared eligibility outcome, not revocation. Ineligible or unresolved cases
can still have administrative demand: the full batch is a private demand preview,
not a claim that unknown or unauthorized work was executed. Unknown allocation
inputs yield null actual/unmet measurements and no complete account batch.
Prior work and reservations use canonical retained postings without spending
again. Completed work can support inspection again; this is not durable case
settlement or replay-safe enforcement.

Results retain the complete request, actual policy findings/rules/provenance and
DOM-02 report with claimant, holder, period, unit, prior/incremental/unmet work and
posting links. Repeated shared availability is context, not additive capacity.
Proposed duties never promise goods/service delivery or external enforcement.
No database, store, clock, callback, accepted revision, policy adoption, spending,
renderer/IPC numerical activation or queue transition is introduced.

[Model card](adjudication-v1.json) describes the hypothetical procedure;
[package record](../../../../docs/roadmap/execution/WP-DOM-04-adjudication.md)
records acceptance. Reviewed legal models, enforcement and agreement mechanisms, other actual
counterparts and parent static/dynamic gates remain pending. No universal
fairness, justice, compliance, legitimacy or conflict severity score is inferred.


## Host exploration and source coverage

`exploration.interview`, `report` and `preview` expose authored explicit case-work
queue alternatives with no default. Retention or reversal affects only existing
case-work slots; unrelated care/governance claims keep their positions. Each
alternative lists its complete candidate claim order. Whole-case capacity uses
the existing evaluator and canonical DOM-02 accounts. Full rights, claimant,
authority, rationale, proposed duties and actual/unmet posting evidence survive.
No legal disposition or authority is chosen by these alternatives.

Edits bind the complete request, canonical world, mechanism/review/implementation
and unit/runtime identities. Changed context rejects old edits. Local interviews
reject competing work in another scope; reports can still inspect such batches.
Requests/results are revalidated and detached. No storage or IPC activation.

`coverage.validate_coverage` reconciles the unactivated
`content/law-conflict/coverage-v1.json` with the pinned society source inventory.
All branches/leaves and source decision alternatives remain visible. Only rights
inspection and finite judicial work have partial hypothetical coverage; legislation,
enforcement, mediation, restoration and external conflict mechanisms retain gaps.
Source maintenance is provenance. See the
[exploration package](../../../../docs/roadmap/execution/WP-DOM-04-options-coverage.md).

The bounded actual DOM-02/04 counterpart consumes this evaluator's case results
and staged finite administration book in
[`economy/adjudication.py`](../economy/adjudication.py). Explicit claimant and
source/entitlement mappings constrain subsequent goods claims without creating
legal entitlement or changing the host's disposition. Case work competes with
independent work over the same dated accounts; unknown cases retain their findings
and withhold the complete candidate. This is private hypothetical conformance,
not settlement or enforcement. See the
[execution record](../../../../docs/roadmap/execution/WP-032-counterparts-economy-adjudication.md).

The DOM-03/04 host in `../governance/adjudication.py` runs actual scoped decisions
before finite case work on the same dated account book. Explicit independent cases
remain independent of approval. Approval preserves host legal assertions and work
terms; it grants no rights, adjudicates no merits and enforces no obligations.
Unknowns and unmet case work remain inspectable; prior work is never refunded.

The actual DOM-04/05 adapter in [`care.py`](care.py) runs case work before
canonical arrangement/beneficiary care on the same dated DOM-02 book. Every duty
explicitly declares a case or independence. A dependent case must match its
beneficiary/target, authority, obligation reference and affected time account.
Upheld preserves original eligibility/access/terms; other known outcomes restrict
only dependent new care. Original-term preflight still validates membership and
terms when the case is unresolved. Findings, unmet duty, prior work, reservations
and population overlap remain inspectable. Reviewed opaque host digests support
common conformance; no duties, rights, enforcement or care outcomes are inferred.
See the [execution record](../../../../docs/roadmap/execution/WP-032-counterparts-adjudication-care.md).

`education.py` composes actual case work and education prerequisites over one
finite canonical dated work book and a disjoint finite facility pool. Every service
maps its case claimant/participant, authority, target, obligations and affected
accounts or explicitly declares independence. Upheld preserves original terms;
other known findings restrict only dependent new commitments. Original preflight,
unknowns and prior commitments stay inspectable. No rights, qualifications,
learning, enforcement, transitions or publication are inferred. Reviewed host
ports carry only context-bound opaque evidence digests.

[`health.py`](health.py) composes actual adjudication and health prerequisites.
Each service declares a case or independence. A dependent case binds its patient,
authority, target, obligations, direct work accounts, required entitlement accounts
and facility accounts. Original preflight validates health terms even when legal
inputs are unresolved. Known non-upheld outcomes restrict only dependent new
commitments; unknowns withhold the candidate. Prior postings and protected holds
remain inspectable, and no treatment, rights or enforcement is inferred. Reviewed
opaque host digests bind the full request/world/units/runtime/review context.
See the [execution record](../../../../docs/roadmap/execution/WP-032-counterparts-adjudication-health.md).

`communication.py` composes actual adjudication and DOM-09 message prerequisites
through the private [adjudication/communication contract](../../contracts/adjudication_communication.py).
Each message explicitly maps a case or declares independence. A dependent case
matches the cohort recipient, channel controller and asset, obligations and finite
channel/verification accounts. Original message preflight remains inspectable.
Upheld preserves original terms; other known findings narrow only new publication
access. Unknowns and incompatible rights withhold the complete candidate. Dated
case administration and message books remain disjoint, retaining prior postings,
holds and subtotals. Reviewed context-bound opaque host ports execute both actual
evaluators through common conformance. No delivery/publication, truth, enforcement,
source activation, scientific review, transitions or public numerical IPC is implied.
