# Finite continuity prerequisites v1

`evaluate_continuity` inspects host-authored response-slot commitments at a
settlement or region and explicit period. Ordinary service, preparedness and
response claims compete in one complete canonical account book. Preparedness
holds use shared reservations; fulfillment consumes the hold once. Existing
postings survive changed declarations. All arithmetic belongs to the shared
allocator and account book; this adapter owns no state and performs no IO.

Canonical institutions own assets and cohorts have separate directed access
relationships. External assets require external-aid claims with explicit
conditions and finite openings. No help is created in response to a shortage.
The host declares the complete competitor book, hazard/exposure provenance,
vulnerability knowledge, authority, disclosure, eligibility and precedence.
These declarations do not resolve jurisdiction or verify actual disclosure.

Institutional delay, disclosure withholding, physical outage, denied access and
finite shortfall have distinct findings. Unknown prerequisites or conflicting
obligations withhold all new allocations. A held reserve remains unavailable to
ordinary claims even when its own claimant is blocked; releasing it is a separate
host decision. Returned candidate books and evidence are detached and private.

A response slot is a hypothetical host-declared service commitment, not delivered
rescue. Staff qualifications, materials, logistics, health, damage and communication
are not integrated by this child. Hazard/exposure references do not establish
probabilities, effects, or causal propagation. There is no universal security score,
automatic rescue, game over, random event, time advance, source activation,
world publication, numerical IPC, parent or cumulative acceptance. Separate calls
on original openings cannot certify cross-domain conservation. Reviewed models,
actual counterpart integration, options/source coverage and deterministic or
stochastic transitions remain later packages.

`exploration.interview` offers explicit retain/reverse queue alternatives with no
default. Private previews bind the complete request, graph, units and adapter/
model/runtime/evaluator identities. Preparedness holds and finite aid conditions
remain unchanged; precedence does not resolve authority or obligations.

`coverage.validate_coverage` reconciles pinned catalog and provenance rows. Partial
emergency response/preparedness/resilience coverage means response slots only.
Technological risks and actual hazard/damage/recovery models remain unsupported.

The bounded DOM-01/12 consumer is
`provisioning.continuity.evaluate_pair`: actual finite response-slot commitments
gate host-declared provisioning phase prerequisites. Known restrictions preserve
their cause; unknown terms withhold the complete candidate. Prior commitments do
not prove current permissions. This adds no hazard, recovery, material creation,
aid delivery, operating-resource model or source activation.

The bounded DOM-03/12 consumer is `governance.continuity.evaluate_pair`. Every
response-slot claim explicitly declares whether approval is required. Decisions
only narrow new access; they preserve original authority/disclosure/aid conditions,
unknowns, conflicts, prior commitments and preparedness holds. Independent canonical
administrative work and finite response-slot books yield one private candidate only
when both resolve. No hazard, rescue, aid creation or operating-resource model is
inferred.
