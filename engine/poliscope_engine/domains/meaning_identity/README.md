# Finite accommodation prerequisites v1

`evaluate_participation` is a pure host-only accommodation eligibility/obligation
procedure. Canonical groups declare distinct commitment/accommodation references,
participation requests, eligibility and access. Canonical institutions control
assets via explicit ownership; directed group-to-asset access is separately
validated. Account scopes are settlement/region and capacity is dated.

All competitors share one canonical finite participation-slot account per asset.
Membership weights never multiply capacity. The host supplies the complete book,
finite amounts, unit definitions and explicit precedence. Missing competitors
cannot be discovered by this adapter; separate calls on original openings do not
certify integration. Capacity represents host-declared accommodation service slots,
not hours, belief quality, actual attendance or satisfaction.

Declined participation, ineligibility, absent/denied access and offline capacity
produce distinct blocking findings. Unknown inputs and conflicting obligations
withhold all new competing commitments. Conflicts remain unresolved for the host;
queue precedence does not resolve rights or duties. Prior postings and reservations
are retained, including when declarations change. Access ceilings and physical
shortages have separate findings. Returned evidence and candidate books are private
and detached; the evaluator performs no IO or world mutation.

Source references are declared provenance, not reviewed empirical evidence. The
card is hypothetical and inactive. No player goal or inherited source ideology
sets a world commitment. No belief, identity, cohesion, legitimacy or purpose
scalar, behavioral effect, attendance, time advance, policy resolution, transition,
source activation, numerical IPC or DOM-11 parent acceptance is inferred.

`exploration.interview` offers explicit retain/reverse queue alternatives with no
default. `preview` binds the complete host request, graph, units and implementation
identities, rejects stale edits and returns a private detached report. Choices
change precedence only; conflicting obligations remain unresolved.

`coverage.validate_coverage` reconciles the standalone pinned DOM-11 catalog and
source/system decisions. Partial pluralism/ritual paths mean service-slot
prerequisites only. Actual governance/law/care/cultural/communication counterparts
and reviewed models retain separate acceptance.

The governance-owned [DOM-03/11 counterpart](../governance/participation.py)
uses actual scoped decisions to restrict only explicitly dependent new access.
Original voluntary participation, eligibility, access ceilings, ownership/reach and
conflicting obligations remain authoritative. Finite accommodation slots retain
prior commitments and reservations; administrative time stays in its canonical
separate book. Unknowns or obligation conflicts withhold the complete private
candidate. No attendance-time/operating-resource interface, belief/identity score,
rights resolution, policy adoption or public IPC is added.
