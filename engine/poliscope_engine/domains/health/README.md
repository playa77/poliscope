# Health service prerequisites v1

`evaluate_health` is a trusted-host pure interface, with no IPC or publication.
`HealthRequest` declares beneficiary care claims, qualified staff paid/unpaid work,
supplies, facility assets, eligibility and policy bindings. Need references are
required; exposure and selected health state remain descriptive references.

Shared `service_capacity.evaluate_prerequisites` also serves education, which
continues to require training instead of beneficiary care. DOM-02 owns work and
goods, the shared allocator owns facility slots, and WP-013 resolves authority.
No domain-specific arithmetic or resource store exists. All competing work and
facility claims must be supplied by the host. One asset has one slot account per
scope/period; pools must be disjoint. Shared staff and beneficiary cohorts retain
one time account. Reports preserve full prior/additional/unmet quantities,
access, reservations and posting evidence, linked to the declared cohort and
scope; no aggregate can create a locally inaccessible service.

`capacity_ready` means all declared inputs are fulfilled and declared eligibility,
qualification and authority are known. It does not mean attendance or treatment.
Denied eligibility and unqualified staff have distinct outcomes. Unknowns and
institutional conflicts withhold readiness. Facility actual placeholders are
non-authoritative when the facility evaluator is unresolved; inspect its status.
Independent input commitments remain visible even when another prerequisite is
blocked. There is no refund or synchronized service delivery transaction.

Hypothetical only; no source model is activated. Population need denominators,
service throughput and treatment coverage, actual infrastructure connectivity,
health/exposure transitions, demographic change and mental/emotional effects are
unavailable. References are host declarations, not empirically certified evidence.
This interface does not complete parent DOM-07, WP-032 or cumulative acceptance.
